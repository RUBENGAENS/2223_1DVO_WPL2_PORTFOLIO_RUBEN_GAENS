boostu branding

Introduction

For boostU, I executed a full-scale rebranding project that spanned from initial concept to final implementation. This included designing a fresh, modern logo and establishing a consistent visual identity that reflects the brand’s vision and values.
Background
Beyond the visual direction, I also designed and developed a fully responsive website, ensuring both aesthetic quality and high performance.

The website was built using Next.js for a fast, scalable frontend experience, combined with Strapi as a flexible headless CMS to enable easy content management. By handling both the creative and technical aspects of the project, I ensured a seamless integration between design and development, resulting in a strong, future-proof digital presence for boostU.
